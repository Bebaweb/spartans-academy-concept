What we promise parents

No big talk.
Just good coaching, every week.

We won't promise your child a professional contract. We do promise qualified coaches who know every player by name, training that suits their age, and a team they'll want to come back to.

  • 01
    Qualified coaches

    Every coach is UEFA-licensed, with professional playing or coaching experience.

  • 02
    Training that fits the child

    Sessions are adapted to each age group and skill level, from first kick to competitive squads.

  • 03
    Open to parents

    You can watch any session. Nothing happens behind closed doors.

  • 04
    More than football

    Teamwork, respect and confidence that children carry off the pitch.

Questions parents ask

Good to know before you start

Something else on your mind? Message us on WhatsApp and a coach will answer.

What ages do you coach?

Boys and girls from 4 to 16, at every level from beginners upward.

Can I stay and watch training?

Yes. Parents are welcome at every session.

How long is the season?

It usually starts in the first week of September and runs to the end of June.

Is the kit included?

Yes, every player receives a full kit for the season.

Do you offer transport?

Transport currently runs from two locations. Contact us to check if yours is covered.

Are there discounts for siblings?

Yes: 30% off for siblings, and 10% off when you bring a new member.

My child wants to be a goalkeeper. Do you train keepers?

Yes, our specialist goalkeeping coach runs separate sessions.
